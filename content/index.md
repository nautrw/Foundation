*The Foundation* is my storage of notes. I'd like to keep it public for ease of access to myself, and perhaps others might find it useful as well. Be warned that not all content may be fully up-to-date, as these are mostly notes for classes I have or am taking.
# Logistics
- Terms that are simply colored lead to other articles within The Foundation.
- Hyperlinked terms (**those that are colored and have an icon next to them**) are linked to external resources that may provide better explanations.
