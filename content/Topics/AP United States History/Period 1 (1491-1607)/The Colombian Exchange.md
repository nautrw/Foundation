The **Columbian exchange** was the transfer of plants, animals, minerals, diseases, and people between the Americas and Europe. 
![the columbian exchange|697](https://mtviewmirror.com/wp-content/uploads/Columbian-Exchange.jpg)[^1]
![The Columbian exchange of crop plants, livestock, and diseases went in both directions between the Old World and the New World.](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/77/BRI_Columbian_Exchange.jpg)[^2]
# Diseases
- People in the Old World had developed immunity to common diseases either of them had because they had frequent contact
- The New World was completely isolated from the Old World, so the natives there had no immunity to those diseases
- Thus, diseases from the Old World were introduced to the New World and killed lots of people
- **Smallpox** was introduced by the Spanish to the Caribbean and spread through Mexico and Central America
	- It killed about half of the indigenous population
- Because so many of the indigenous slaves died, African slaves were introduced to Latin America to replace them.
# Food & Plants
- Europeans introduced wheat, olives, grapes, rice, and sugar to the New World
- [[Maize]], tomatoes, and potatoes were brought to the Old World
- Africans brought [Okra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okra) with them to South America, and it became a staple food
- The surplus of nutrient-rich foods in Europe lead to a massive increase in public health and a population boom
# Animals
- Europeans introduced horses, sheep, cattle, and cows to the New World
- The introduction of the horse was very important
	- Specially in the [[Major Cultural Regions of North America#Great Basin, Great Plains, and Southwest|great plains]], allowed for much higher amounts of hunting and new farming practices like plowing
	- This also increased the amount of violent conflicts in the region
# Minerals
- **Gold** and **silver** were the main minerals extracted from the New World by the Spanish
# People
- There were small amounts of indigenous Americans brought to the Old World
- Millions of enslaved Africans were transferred to colonies in the New World in order to work in cash crop plantations
# Other
- **Firearms** and **gunpowder** were introduced to the Americas from Europe
	- Caused arms races and changes in power dynamics between tribes
	- All groups involved got more military power
- **Rubber** was introduced from the Americas to Europe
	- Allowed for many goods like waterproof clothing
