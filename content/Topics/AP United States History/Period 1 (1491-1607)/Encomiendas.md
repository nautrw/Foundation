**Encomiendas** were a labor system enforced by the Spanish in which land was given to Spanish colonists (*encomenderos*) and indigenous people were forced to work in plantations and mines. The colonists were supposed to provide protection and Christian education, but, in practice, it just became slavery. Cash crops like sugarcane and tobacco were grown.

It was abolished in 1542 and replaced with a less harsh system.
# Bartolomé de las Casas
**Bartolomé de las Casas** was an early Spanish colonist and Roman Catholic priest that witnessed many of the abuses of the encomienda system and advocated for the Native Americans. He published a book called *A Short Account of the Destruction of the Indies*, which detailed many of the abuses against the Native Americans.
