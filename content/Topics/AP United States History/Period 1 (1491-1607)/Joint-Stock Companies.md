- **Joint-Stack companies** were formed by groups of private investors pooling their money
	- Investors bought shares to split the risk
	- Profits were split along investors according to the amount of shares they owned
	- If the colony got rich, everyone profited, but if it failed, no one was ruined
# The Virginia Company
- The **Virginia Company** was charted by the English monarch in 1606
	- Allowed the English king to not have to pay for [[Topics/AP United States History/Period 1 (1491-1607)/Early English Colonization|colonization]] like the [[Topics/AP United States History/Period 1 (1491-1607)/Spanish Exploration in the Americas|Spanish]]
	- Mostly known for founding [[Early English Colonization#Jamestown|Jamestown]]
