**Maize/corn** allowed the expansion of Native [[Topics/AP United States History/Period 1 (1491-1607)/Major Cultural Regions of North America|American societies]]. It started being cultivated in Southern Mexico in 5,000 B.C. It was spread through the Americas by trade and migration routes.
# Effects
## Economic Development
- Trade of crops allowed for more economic growth
- A surplus of food allowed people to specialize in other areas, further boosting economic development
## Permanent Settlements
- Allowed the transition from nomadic hunter-gatherers to sedentary agricultural [[Topics/AP United States History/Period 1 (1491-1607)/Major Cultural Regions of North America|societies]]
- Formation of agricultural [[Topics/AP United States History/Period 1 (1491-1607)/Major Cultural Regions of North America|societies]] caused the formation of social and political structures
## Irrigation Technology
- Many Native [[Topics/AP United States History/Period 1 (1491-1607)/Major Cultural Regions of North America|American societies]] relied on irrigation technology to bring water to the fields of maize because they had very little rainfall
