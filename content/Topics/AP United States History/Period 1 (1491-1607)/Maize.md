**Maize/corn** allowed the expansion of Native American societies. It started being cultivated in Southern Mexico in 5,000 B.C. It was spread through the Americas by trade and migration routes.
# Effects
## Economic Development
- Trade of crops allowed for more economic growth
- A surplus of food allowed people to specialize in other areas, further boosting economic development
## Permanent Settlements
- Allowed the transition from nomadic hunter-gatherers to sedentary agricultural societies
- Formation of agricultural societies caused the formation of social and political structures
## Irrigation Technology
- Many Native American societies relied on irrigation technology to bring water to the fields of maize because they had very little rainfall
