- The **triangular trade** was a system of trade between New England, West Indies, and West Africa
- New England shipped Rum to West Africa
- West Africa shipped slaves to the West Indies
	- Transported via the **middle passage**, which had brutal conditions and killed 10-20% of those that were transported
- The West Indies would send Sugarcane to New England
![triangular slave trade](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/46/Triangular_trade.jpg)[^1]
- Introduced metal tools, firearms, and cloth to native societies
- Continued the spread of [[The Colombian Exchange#Diseases|diseases]] like smallpox
