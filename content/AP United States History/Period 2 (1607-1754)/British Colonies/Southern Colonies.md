- The highest concentration of enslaved labor
- Imported a lot of slaves from the [[Transatlantic Trade]]

# Chesapeake [[AP United States History/Period 2 (1607-1754)/Spanish Colonies|Colonies]]
- A subset of the Southern Colonies
- Composed of Virginia and Maryland
- Had lots of fertile soil, making tobacco very profitable
- A lot of labor came from indentured servants
## Virginia
- Founded in 1607 as a [[Types of Colonies|charter/corporate]] colony operated by [[Joint-Stock Companies#The Virginia Company|The Virginia Company]]
- Started out as [[Early English Colonization#Jamestown|Jamestown]]
- **John Rolfe** and **Pocahontas** grew tobacco for cash
- Became the first [[Types of Colonies|royal]] colony in 1624 when the British government took over
	- Many people had died from disease and conflicts with native Americans
	- [[Joint-Stock Companies#The Virginia Company|The Virginia Company]] was bankrupt
	- James I revoked their charter and took over
- Established the **House of Burgesses** in 1619
	- The first representative assembly in America
	- White male landowners called burgesses were elected to pass laws on taxes, defense, and local affairs
	- Voting was limited to male landowners
- **Headright system** gave 50 acres of land to anybody who could pay to transport themselves of other
	- Allowed settlers to get massive amounts of land
	- Caused lots of indentured servants to live in Virginia
### Gloucester Slave Rebellion
- Also known as the **Servant's plot** and **Birkenhead's Rebellion**
- One of the first slave rebellions in America (1663)
- Caused by bad living and working conditions for indentured servants and enslaved people
- It was planned in meetings but one of the slaves who attended told the governor and prevented the whole thing
### Bacon's Rebellion
- **Bacon's Rebellion** was led by **Nathaniel Bacon** against the governor in 1676
	- They felt the government wasn't doing enough to protect them from natives
	- Poor farmers had no power in the house of Burgesses
	- [[AP United States History/Period 1 (1491-1607)/Early English Colonization|Jamestown]] was torched and the governor was driven out
	- Caused farmers to import enslaved Africans instead of using indentured servants
## Maryland
- **[[AP United States History/Period 2 (1607-1754)/British Colonies/Types of Colonies|Proprietary]]** colony founded by **George Calvert** (or **Lord Baltimore**) in 1632
- Passed down to **Cecil Calvert** in 1634
- Meant to be a safe haven for Catholics
- **Act of Toleration** of 1649 grants religious freedom to all Christian regardless of denomination
	- Called for the death of anybody who wasn't Christian
	- Causes the **Protestant revolt** in which Protestants ignited a civil war in the late 1600s to repeal the Act of Toleration
# Carolinas
- The **Province of Carolina** was a colony established in 1663
- Originally granted to 8 nobles and was a [[Types of Colonies|proprietary]] colony
- ==Split into North and South Carolina in 1712 but did not become separate [[Types of Colonies|royal]] colonies until 1729 when the king purchased the rights==
## North Carolina
- Founded in 1712 after Carolina was split
- Early attempts at settlement were unsuccessful ([[Early English Colonization#Roanoke Colony|Roanoke Colony]] was close to here)
## South Carolina
- Founded in 1712 after Carolina was split
- Grew rice, cotton, tobacco, and indigo
- Used lots of slavery
### Stono Rebellion
- In 1739, enslaved workers in South Carolina rebelled and killed dozens of people
- Liberty is promised in [[AP United States History/Period 2 (1607-1754)/Spanish Colonies|Spanish Florida]] so the slaves tried to escape there
- Caused colonies to pass stricter slave codes ([Negro Act of 1740](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negro_Act_of_1740))
	- It was now forbidden for slaves to meet in groups, move abroad, earn money, learn to write, and more 
# Georgia
- Founded in 1732 by **James Oglethorpe**
- A [[Types of Colonies|proprietary]] colony at first (then a [[Types of Colonies|royal]] colony in 1752)
- The last of the 13 colonies
- Used as a defensive buffer between South Carolina and [[AP United States History/Period 2 (1607-1754)/Spanish Colonies|Spanish Florida]]
- Thousands of people imprisoned for debt were sent here to relieve the jails and let them start over
- Did not prosper at first so the British government took over and turned it into a [[Types of Colonies|royal]] colony in 1752
