# Spain
- Sent many colonists
- Main goal was [[Spanish Exploration in the Americas#Gold (Economic Development)|wealth extraction]]
	- Originally from the extraction of [[The Colombian Exchange#Minerals|minerals like gold and silver]] but later of the farming of cash crops
	- [[Encomiendas|Encomienda]] system subjugates native populations into slave labor
- Missionaries sent to convert the natives to Catholicism
- [[Spanish Exploration in the Americas#Gold (Economic Development)|Caste system]] established to divide the societies by race
- See [[Spanish Colonies]]
# France
- Sent few colonists but many traders
- Main goal was to build trading posts and form economic alliances
	- Heavily relied on rivers
	- **Fur** was in high demand so fur trappers used their connections to get more fur to meet demand
		- Fur trappers married the natives to get trading rights for more fur
- Very few missionaries sent (main goal was economic)
# Dutch
- Sent few colonists but many traders
- Wanted a water-based passage through the Americas
- Founded **New Amsterdam** (later [[Middle Colonies#New York|New York]]), which attracted a lot of merchants
- Weren't interested in converting the natives
# British
- Sent many colonists
- Colonies were primarily established for economic gain
- Many civilians moved to the colonies for different purposes
	- Did so in family groups
	- [[Religious Groups from England|Religious groups]] seeking religious freedom from England
	- More opportunities in the colonies attracted people looking for social mobility
		- [Enclosure movement](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/economics/enclosure-movement) pushed out poor farmers
- Farmed a lot and claimed land from the natives
- Did not intermarry with the natives
