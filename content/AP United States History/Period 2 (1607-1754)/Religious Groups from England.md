# Puritans
- The **Puritans** were a group of Protestant Christians that thought the Church of England could be purified
- More moderate than the Pilgrims
- Gained a charter from the king in 1629 to found [[New England Colonies#Massachusetts Bay Colony|Massachusetts Bay Colony]]
# Pilgrims
- Originally known as the **separatists**
- Wanted to separate from the Church of England entirely
- **King James I** persecuted them for speaking out
- Initially fled to the Netherlands before migrating to the Americas
# Quakers
- Also known as **The Religious Society of Friends**
- Believed that religious authority came from the person and not from the Bible or other outside sources
- Supported equality among men and women
- Resisted military service
- Persecuted in England
