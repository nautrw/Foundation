# Powhatan Uprising
- The Powhatan tribe led a surprise attack against the settlers in [[AP United States History/Period 1 (1491-1607)/Early English Colonization|Jamestown]] in 1622
- 347 settlers died
- Caused by the colonies expanding onto Powhatan lands for tobacco farming land
# Pequot War
- Violent conflict between [[AP United States History/Period 2 (1607-1754)/British Colonies/New England Colonies|New England colonies]] and the Pequot nation between 1636 and 1638
- Mostly happened in [[AP United States History/Period 2 (1607-1754)/British Colonies/New England Colonies|Connecticut]]
- Caused by increasing [[AP United States History/Period 2 (1607-1754)/Religious Groups from England|Puritan]] populations, tensions over the fur trade, and the killing of English traders by native Americans
- Ended in the defeat of the Pequots
- The **Mystic massacre** of 1637 was when [[AP United States History/Period 2 (1607-1754)/British Colonies/New England Colonies|Connecticut]] colonists burned down the Pequot fort and killed ~700 people
	- Almost wiped out the Pequots
# King Philip's War
- Also known as **Metacom's War**
- Went from 1675-1676
- Caused by treaty violations by settlers in [[New England Colonies#Massachusetts|Massachusetts]]
	- They kept expanding and taking away native American lands
- **Metacom** was the chief of the Wampanoag tribe (he was known as **King Philip** by the colonists)
	- He united many tribes together and led a rebellion against the colonists
- Resulted in the death of thousands of people and the colonists winning the war with their native American allies
	- Metacom died
	- Known as the end of native American resistance in New England
# Pueblo Revolt
- Happened in 1680
- Also known as **Popé's rebellion**
- Afailed rebellion in which the Pueblo people killed Spaniards and burned churches
	- Causes:
		- Forced conversion to Catholicism
		- Missionaries burned sacred objects
		- Severe punishment by Spanish courts (whipping, hanging, dismemberment)
		- Slavery
- Resulted in the Spanish being driven out of New Mexico for a decade
	- This caused the Spanish to tolerated the Pueblo people a little bit more
	- People began to intermarry and syncretize
