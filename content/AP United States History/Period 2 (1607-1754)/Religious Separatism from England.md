# Puritans
- The **Puritans** were a group of Protestant Christians that wanted to stay in England but change the Church of England
# Pilgrims
- Originally known as the **separatists**
- Wanted to separate from the Church of England entirely
- **King James I** persecuted them for speaking out
- Initially fled to the Netherlands before migrating to the Americas
