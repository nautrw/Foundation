## Plymouth Colony
- Founded in 1620 by a group of [[Religious Separatism from England#Pilgrims|Pilgrims]]
- Emigrated as family groups that set out to make a new society
- The **Mayflower compact** is an agreement by the adult male settlers aboard the Mayflower that establishes a simple majority-rule government
	- Women, servants, and people that weren't part of the church had no say in government
# Massachusetts Bay Colony
- Founded in 1629 by a group of [[Religious Separatism from England#Puritans|Puritans]] led by John Winthrop
- Settled by family groups
- Wanted a society based on Biblical principles
- All free men that owned land could vote on policy
	- There was a bigger proportion of eligible voters than in England
	- There was a town hall to debate policy
	- Majority rule
