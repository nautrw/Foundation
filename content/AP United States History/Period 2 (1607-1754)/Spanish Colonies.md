# New Spain
- **New Spain** was the area colonized by the Spanish, including central America and the Philippines
![new spain](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/57/Map_of_the_Viceroyalty_of_New_Spain.svg/1920px-Map_of_the_Viceroyalty_of_New_Spain.svg.png)[^2]
# Florida
- Claimed by Spain in 1513 (they sent **Juan Ponce de Léon**)
- Spain established the permanent settlement of **St. Augustine** in 1565
- Little silver and gold, so not many settlements
![map of spanish florida](https://pages.uwf.edu/jworth/SpanishFlorida_1587-1706.jpg)[^1]
# New Mexico & Arizona
- Spain arrived in 1598
- **Santa Fe** was established by Spain in 1610 as the capital of New Mexico
# Texas
- Spain established settlements in Texas from 1519 to 1821
- Grew in the early 1700s
# California
- **San Diego** established in 1769
- Missionaries sent along the coast in 1784
