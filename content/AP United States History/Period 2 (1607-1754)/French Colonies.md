# Quebec
- First French settlement in America
- Located on the St. Lawrence River
- Founded in 1608
# Louisiana
- France sent explorers to the [[AP United States History/Period 1 (1491-1607)/Major Cultural Regions of North America|Mississippi]] region in 1683
- Claimed for France in 1682
- Became an actual French colony in 1699
# New Orleans 
- Established in 1718 where the [[AP United States History/Period 1 (1491-1607)/Major Cultural Regions of North America|Mississippi]] river entered the Gulf of Mexico
- Trading center
