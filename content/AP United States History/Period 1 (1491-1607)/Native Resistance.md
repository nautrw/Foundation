Most Native American tribes sought to keep their independence and way of live after [[Spanish Exploration in the Americas|Europeans came to the Americas]].
# Alliances
- Some tribes formed alliances to protect each other against invasion
## Iroquois Confederacy
- The **Iroquois Confederacy/League**, also known as the **Haudenosaunee Confederacy** was an alliance of 5 (later 6) tribes against invasion
	- Located across what is now upper New York
	- Time it was founded is unknown, likely late 1500s
	- Banded together to exterminate the rival tribes
	- Sided with the British in the [[American Revolution]] and fell apart when they lost
	- See also [[Major Cultural Regions of North America#Northeast|Northeastern Cultures]]
	- Included:
		- Seneca
		- Cayuga
		- Onondaga
		- Oneida
		- Mohawk
		- The **Tuscarora** joined later in 1722 as the 6th tribe

# Rebellions
- Other tribes outright rebelled against colonists
- The **Pueblo rebellion** of 1680 was a failed rebellion in which the Pueblo people killed Spaniards and burned churches
	- Causes:
		- Forced conversion to Catholicism
		- Missionaries burned sacred objects
		- Severe punishment by Spanish courts (whipping, hanging, dismemberment)
		- Slavery
	- This caused the Spanish to tolerated the Pueblo people a little bit more
	- People began to intermarry and syncretize
