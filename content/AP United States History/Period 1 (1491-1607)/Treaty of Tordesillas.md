Portugal was worried that Spain was expanding too rapidly and getting far ahead of them, so they almost attacked Spain. *Pope Alexander VI* interceded and drew a line of demarcation splitting Latin America between Portugal and Spain in 1493.

The **treaty of Tordesillas** was signed in 1494, in which both empires ratified/recognized the demarcation. It gave Portugal a bit more land. Because most of Latin America was still unexplored, it was very imbalanced and Spain still got most of the land.
![Lines dividing the non-Christian world between Castile and Portugal: the 1494 Tordesillas meridian (purple) and the 1529 Zaragoza antimeridian (green)](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/21/Spain_and_Portugal.png)[^1]

It is important to note that the natives of the region did not have a say in this split.
