1. Starting with:
$$
ax^{2}+bx+c=0
$$
2. Remove $c$ from both sides:
$$
\begin{aligned}
ax^2+bx+c\textcolor{red}{-c}&=0\textcolor{red}{-c} \\
ax^2+bx&=-c
\end{aligned}
$$
3. Remove the $a$ from the left side
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{ax^{2}+bx}{\textcolor{red}a}&=\frac{-c}{\textcolor{red}{a}} \\
x^{2}+\frac{b}{a}x&=\frac{-c}{a}
\end{aligned}
$$
4. Divide the coefficient of the linear term ($\frac{b}{a}$)  by 2 and raise it to the 2nd power
$$
\left(\frac{\frac{b}{a}}{2}\right)^{2} = \left(\frac{b}{2a}\right)^{2}=\frac{b^2}{4a^{2}}
$$
5. Add it to both sides of the equation
$$
x^{2}+\frac{b}{a}x\textcolor{red}{+\frac{b^{2}}{4a^{2}}}=\frac{-c}{a}\textcolor{red}{+\frac{b^{2}}{4a^{2}}}
$$
6. Simplify the right side by finding the common denominator ($4a^2$)
$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{2}+\frac{b}{a}x+\frac{b^{2}}{4a^{2}}&=\frac{-c}{a}\textcolor{red}{\left(\frac{4a}{4a}\right)}+\frac{b^2}{4a^2}\\
&=\frac{-4ac}{4a^2}+\frac{b^2}{4a^2}\\
&=\frac{b^2-4ac}{4a^2}\\
x^{2}+\frac{b}{a}x+\frac{b^{2}}{4a^{2}}&=\frac{b^2-4ac}{4a^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
7. Simplify the perfect square trinomial on the left side into its factored form
$$
\left(x+\frac{b}{2a}\right)^2=\frac{b^2-4ac}{4a^2}
$$
8. Take the square root of both sides to eliminate the square on the left
$$
\sqrt{\left(x+\frac{b}{2a}\right)^2}=\sqrt{\frac{b^{2}-4ac}{4a^{2}}}
$$
9. Simplify the left side
$$
x+\frac{b}{2a}=\sqrt{\frac{b^{2}-4ac}{4a^{2}}}
$$
10. Add the plus-minus sign to the right side of the equation because there are 2 solutions
$$
x+\frac{b}{2a}=\pm\sqrt{\frac{b^{2}-4ac}{4a^{2}}}
$$
11. Isolate the $x$ on the left by subtracting both sides by $\frac{b}{2a}$
$$
x+\frac{b}{2a}\textcolor{red}{-\frac{b}{2a}}=\pm\frac{\sqrt{b^{2}-4ac}}{2a}\textcolor{red}{-\frac{b}{2a}}
$$
12. Simplify
$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=\frac{-b}{2a}\pm\sqrt{\frac{b^{2}-4ac}{2a}} \\
x&=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}
\end{aligned}
$$
And done!
$$
ax^{2}+bx+c=0\rightarrow x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^{2}-4ac}}{2a}
$$
